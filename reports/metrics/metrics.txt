5. Summary
==================================================

The Cinema Ticket Management System contains 1,797 physical
lines of production Java code and 82 methods.

The average physical size is approximately 21.91 lines per
method.

The estimated total cyclomatic complexity is 134, based on
52 identified decision points and a base complexity of one
for each method.

The total class-level efferent coupling is 30. MoviePanel has
the highest efferent coupling because it coordinates the GUI
with movie models, services, staff and exception classes.

Overall, the metrics provide an indication of the size,
structural complexity and inter-class dependencies of the
Cinema Ticket Management System.
